HACKATHON CHALLENGE
BTU AI Skills Digital Passport
The Challenge
The Business and Technology University (BTU) is creating the "BTU AI Skills Digital Passport"—a digital record of AI skills acquired by students and verified by the university.
Unlike a standard certificate, the AI ​​Skills Digital Passport is a persistent document with a permanent, unique ID; its content expands incrementally as the student’s skills develop.
Whenever a student acquires a new AI skill, the corresponding skill, badge, date of acquisition, and description are added to the passport, while existing information remains preserved.
Your challenge for this hackathon is to develop a working prototype (MVP) of a technical solution that automates the management of the BTU AI Skills Digital Passport and enables the verification of its contents via blockchain.
________________


What are you expected to build?
The system you develop must facilitate the automated generation of a student’s Digital Passport, its periodic updates, and the verification of its authenticity on the blockchain.
BTU will provide the teams with:
* A complete, multi-page visual design for the Digital Passport;
* A database of test student data. * The structure of Skills and Badges.
________________


Mandatory Functional Requirements
1. Unique Digital Passport
A single Digital Passport must be created for each student, and a permanent, unique Passport ID must be assigned.
The student’s first name, last name, Passport ID, and the initial issuance date are recorded at the time of the Passport's creation and remain unchanged during any subsequent updates.
The Digital Passport shall be a single, multi-page PDF document.
2. Integration with Google Sheets
Google Sheets serves as the primary data source for the system.
When an administrator adds a new verified Skill for a student, the system must be able to detect this change, associate it with the relevant student, and update their existing Digital Passport.
3. Automated Passport Generation
The system must automatically populate a visual template—provided by BTU—with the relevant student's data to generate the final multi-page PDF.
The process must not require manual assembly of documents for individual students.
4. Adding New Skills
When a student acquires a new AI-related skill, the following information must be added to their existing Passport:
* The name of the skill;
* The corresponding Badge/Stamp;
* The date the skill was acquired;
* A relevant description. When a new skill is added, a new passport is not created for the student; instead, their existing Digital Passport is updated.
5. Blockchain Verification – Mandatory Requirement
The authenticity of every officially issued version of a Digital Passport must be verifiable on the blockchain.
Blockchain verification must confirm that:
* The document was officially issued;
* The presented document matches the officially issued version;
* Its content has not been altered since issuance.
If even a single piece of data in the PDF is changed after issuance—such as the student's name, a skill, a badge, or a date—the altered document must fail blockchain verification.
Teams are free to select an appropriate blockchain network, protocol, or technological solution.
6. Verification of the Issuer’s Identity (BTU)
Verification must go beyond merely confirming that the document remains unaltered.
The solution must enable third parties to verify the identity of BTU as the official issuer of the Digital Passport.
Consequently, the final verification process must confirm two key components:
Document Authenticity – the document is genuine and unaltered; Issuer Authenticity – BTU is the verified issuer of the document.
7. Unique Verification Code/ID
The Digital Passport must include a unique Verification Code/ID, allowing any interested party to verify the document.
For example, an employer can enter the verification code/ID found on the passport into a designated field within the relevant system or website to confirm its authenticity.
Third parties should not be required to register or log in to perform verification.
8. Passport Updates and Version Management
The Digital Passport may be updated over time.
Following each update, the system must be able to properly record the new version and verify it via blockchain.
Throughout this process, the student’s original, primary Passport ID must remain unchanged.
The system must be capable of identifying the latest valid version of the Passport.
9. Student Access
Students must be able to view and download their own Digital Passports.
Ideally, access to the student’s personal document should be granted using their BTU university email address or another secure authentication mechanism.
Public verification of the document, however, must be possible without the need for authorization. 10. Scalability
The solution must be designed to support the issuance and subsequent management of at least 5,000 Digital Passports per year, with the potential for further growth.
Particular preference will be given to solutions that ensure maximum process automation and do not require individual, manual processing of each document.


Ultimate Goal of the Challenge
Develop a solution that enables BTU to automatically manage a single, persistent, incrementally updatable, and blockchain-verified "AI Skills Digital Passport" for thousands of students, while allowing students, employers, or any other interested party to easily and independently verify its authenticity.
